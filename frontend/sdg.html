SDG 9 · Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure

Unified Digital Infrastructure

Blueprint 01 / 03
Web Portal Architecture

Both campus kitchens—Cafe by the Valley (78 items) and Atmosphere Cafe (72 items)—share one responsive web backbone with synchronized order state.

Official Focus · UN SDG 9

“Build resilient infrastructure, promote inclusive and sustainable industrialization, and foster innovation.”

How Food-Verse Aligns: Replaces fragmented, manual counter-based ordering with a unified campus digital platform. Connects all campus restaurants under one web architecture with a centralized Admin & Restaurant Management Dashboard, digital menus, and real-time order status workflows.

01 · Unified Multi-Outlet Web Architecture Connects all campus restaurants under one clean portal with persistent cross-kitchen bag storage.
02 · Centralized Admin & Restaurant Dashboard Manages digital menus, customization choices, and kitchen dispatch details centrally.
03 · Real-Time Order Status Workflows Structured preparation states move every order cleanly from checkout to counter pickup or delivery.

SDG 12 · Responsible Consumption & Production

Zero-Waste Demand Visibility

Blueprint 02 / 03
Scheduled Demand Signals

Pre-scheduled pickup slots alert kitchens ahead of class breaks so cooks prepare accurate portions rather than guessing.

Official Focus · UN SDG 12

“Ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns.”

How Food-Verse Aligns: Pre-scheduled pickup time slots and digital pre-ordering give campus kitchens clearer demand visibility, helping reduce over-preparation and food waste. Digital order confirmations and in-app notifications also eliminate paper order slips and receipts.

01 · Advance Break-Slot Pre-Ordering Gives campus kitchens early demand visibility before peak lecture breaks arrive.
02 · Demand-Matched Kitchen Preparation Reduces over-preparation and end-of-day food waste across both campus outlets.
03 · Digital Confirmations & Alerts Eliminates paper order slips and receipts through instant on-screen verification.

SDG 3 · Good Health & Well-being

Calm, Timely Mealtime Care

Blueprint 03 / 03
No Skipped Meals

Pre-ordering before breaks lets students eat proper, nutritious meals between classes instead of skipping lunch when counters back up.

Official Focus · UN SDG 3

“Ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all at all ages.”

How Food-Verse Aligns: Eliminates stressful, rushed lunch breaks and long waiting queues between classes so students and staff can consistently eat nutritious meals on time instead of skipping meals. Reduces physical overcrowding in dining areas during peak hours.

01 · Queue-Free Lecture Break Transitions Removes waiting bottlenecks between classes so lunch breaks stay calm and unhurried.
02 · Consistent Nutritious Meal Schedules Prevents skipped meals caused by tight break windows and unpredictable counter wait times.
03 · Reduced Peak-Hour Overcrowding Keeps campus dining spaces calmer, cleaner, and physically less congested at lunchtime.
